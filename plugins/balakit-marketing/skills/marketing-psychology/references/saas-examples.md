# SaaS Worked Examples (`AcmeCall`)

Concrete illustrations of the decision model in `marketing-psychology` applied
to an illustrative small-business SaaS (`AcmeCall` — a missed-call recovery
service for local service businesses).

## Product Context

Small business owners are time-constrained and skeptical of complex software.
Every surface must reduce cognitive load and demonstrate immediate value.

## Key Models Mapped to Product Surfaces

- **Jobs to Be Done**: Owners "hire" the product to protect revenue from missed calls while on a job site.
- **Immediate "Wow" Moment**: A live "Test Call Now" step during onboarding proves value within 30 seconds (Reciprocity & Mere Exposure).
- **Friction Reduction**: Pre-filling public business hours and phone metadata avoids blank-form fatigue (IKEA Effect — seeing their real shop pre-configured).

## Behavioral Levers (Ethical Only)

- **Social Proof**: Verified customer quotes by trade in the `ReviewsWall` component.
- **Real Urgency**: A transparent "7-Day Missed-Call Audit" trial window with neutral opt-out copy.
- **Loss Aversion**: Frame missed calls with realistic local job-value estimates rather than inflated claims.
- **Anchoring**: Contrast the flagship plan against the cost of a single missed service appointment.
- **Zeigarnik Effect**: Surface "Unreturned Leads" in the dashboard header with one-click follow-up.

## Surface-to-Principle Matrix

| Product surface | Primary principle | Secondary principle |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding flow | IKEA effect | Avoid paradox of choice |
| Pricing tier comparison | Anchoring | Loss aversion |
| `ReviewsWall` component | Social proof | Reciprocity |
| Trial "7-Day Audit" nudge | Real timebox | Commitment & consistency |
| Dashboard "Unreturned leads" | Zeigarnik effect | Loss aversion |
| "Test Call Now" step | Reciprocity | Fogg: high ability + clear prompt |
